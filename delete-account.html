Delete your AboBake account

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Deleting is permanent. It cannot be undone, and we can't bring your records back afterwards.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open AboBake and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile on the bottom bar, then scroll down to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email (if you can't open the app)

Email hillsbay39@gmail.com from the email address you use in AboBake, with the subject Delete my AboBake account. We'll delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. If you signed in with Google, tell us the Google email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email, Google link and sessions) and bakery profile.
  • Products, recipes and product photos.
  • Ingredient stock, stock history, supplier bills, purchase lists and ingredient prices.
  • Production plans, batches, templates and waste.
  • Sales, expenses and cash counts.
  • Customer orders, regular orders, shop sales and customer dues.
  • Staff records, attendance, advances and payments.
  • Your notification address and AI usage counts.

What is not deleted

  • Your Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) first, or you may keep being billed.
  • Anything you shared outside the app, such as a report sent on WhatsApp.
  • Copies in our providers' routine backups, which are removed as those backups expire.
  • Records we must keep by law, if any.

Want a copy first?

Premium accounts can use Profile → Backup to download all records as Excel files before deleting.
