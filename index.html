Better Bakery. Smarter Business.

The app for running a small bakery. Know what every product really costs, plan the day's baking, keep stock under control, take orders, and see your sales and profit, all from your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play
🧮

Cost and price

Cost every recipe from your own ingredient prices. Profit, wholesale and MRP calculators, with tax and a recipe scaler.

🍞

Your recipes

Keep your recipes with photos, cost per piece and batch sizes. Import them from a spreadsheet or a photo.

👩‍🍳

Daily production

Plan what to bake from your sales, follow each batch through its stages, and log waste.

📦

Stock that keeps up

Count stock, record usage, scan a supplier bill, and get a shopping list before you run out.

🎂

Orders and customers

Take customer orders, regular orders and shop sales. See who owes you, and get pickup reminders.

📈

Money and reports

Sales, expenses, cash closing, staff wages and monthly reports, ready to share on WhatsApp.

✨

AboBake AI

Ask about your own sales, stock and orders in English or Hindi, and log a sale or expense by typing it.

Built for India

Rupee amounts in Indian format, UPI QR for payments, Hindi and English, and the products you make: pav, bread, rusk, cookies and cakes.
