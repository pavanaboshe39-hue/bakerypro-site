Common questions

I can't sign in or forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password and follow the steps. If you signed up with Google, use Continue with Google with the same Google account.

My changes don't show on another phone

Make sure you are signed in to the same account on both phones and connected to the internet. Guest mode keeps data on the one phone only.

How do I get the latest update?

Open the app. When a Restart banner appears, tap it. For bigger updates, update AboBake in the Google Play Store.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open the Google Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → AboBake → Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. If you've lost access after paying, open Profile → Subscription → Restore purchases.

How do I take a copy of my records?

Premium accounts: Profile → Backup downloads all your records as Excel files, and Reports → Export sales downloads your sales. Any account can share a report on WhatsApp.
