Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains what information the BakeryPro mobile app ("BakeryPro", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using BakeryPro you agree to this policy.

In short: we only collect what the app needs to work: your sign-in details and the bakery records you enter. We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't use it to track you across other apps.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email and password: if you sign up with email, we store your email address. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider; we can't see it.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We don't get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Bakery profile: your name and your bakery's name, if you enter them, so the app can greet you and put the bakery name on reports.

Bakery records you enter

Everything you add in the app so it can do its job: your products and recipes, ingredient stock and prices, production plans, batches, waste, and sales (product names, quantities, prices, costs and dates).

Product photos

If you add a photo to a product, the app asks for permission to use your camera or photo library, and uploads only the photo you pick. Photos are stored at a web address that is hard to guess; anyone who has that exact address could view the photo, so please don't use photos that show private information.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to BakeryPro Premium, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. Our subscription provider receives a random account ID and your purchase status so the app knows which features to unlock.

Information that stays on your phone

If you use the app as a guest, the bakery profile you enter is saved only on your phone. The app also stores small settings on your phone, such as whether you've finished the welcome screens and your sign-in session.

We don't collect your location, contacts, SMS, call logs or device advertising ID, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To save your bakery records and show them back to you: costs, plans, stock, reports and charts.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as password reset codes.
  • To unlock Premium features when you have an active subscription.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We only share it with the service providers that run parts of the app for us, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase: stores your account, your bakery records and product photos, and handles sign-in.
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you use it), the Gmail service that sends our account emails, and Google Play for payments.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of your subscription status.

When you use "Share on WhatsApp" or "Share another way", the report is sent only to the app and people you choose. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and bakery records for as long as your account exists, so they're there when you come back. When you delete your account in the app, your account, records and photos are deleted straight away; requests by email are handled within 30 days.

5. Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account in the app at any time: More → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login and everything linked to it: products, recipes, product photos, stock, production records and sales.

If you can't use the app, email us at pavanaboshe39@gmail.com from the email address you use in the app, with the subject "Delete my BakeryPro account", and we'll delete it within 30 days. You can also delete individual products and records in the app at any time.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

6. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can only read its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

7. Children

BakeryPro is a business tool meant for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing us. We'll reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is important.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email pavanaboshe39@gmail.com.
