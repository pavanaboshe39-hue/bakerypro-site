Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the AboBake mobile app ("AboBake", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using AboBake you agree to this policy. The Terms of use explain the rules for using the app.

In short: we only collect what the app needs to work: your sign-in details and the bakery records you enter. We don't show ads, we don't sell your information, and we don't use it to track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email and password: if you sign up with email, we store your email address. Your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider; we can't see it.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We don't get your Google password or access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data.
  • Bakery profile: your name, your bakery's name and, if you enter it, your UPI ID, so the app can greet you, put the bakery name on reports and show your payment QR. AboBake does not take or process payments; a UPI payment goes straight from your customer's app to you.

Bakery records you enter

Everything you add in the app so it can do its job:

  • products, recipes, ingredient stock, prices and stock history;
  • supplier names, phone numbers and the supplier bills and purchase lists you save;
  • production plans, batches and waste;
  • sales, business expenses and daily cash counts (amounts, dates, categories and any note);
  • your price list and any customer dues.

Your customers' details

If you use Customer orders, Regular orders, Shop sale or Customer dues, the app stores what you type: the customer's or shop's name, their phone number (optional), what they ordered, the pickup date, prices, payments and notes. You're responsible for having your customers' permission to keep these details. We only use them to show your records back to you; the app never contacts your customers by itself. Messages and calls only happen when you tap WhatsApp or Call, and go through those apps on your phone.

Your staff's details

If you use Staff & wages, the app stores each worker's name, optional phone number, pay rate, attendance, advances and payments that you enter. Please make sure your staff know you keep these records.

AboBake AI and photo reading (Premium)

  • Questions: if you use AboBake AI, the questions you type, the conversation so far, and the parts of your bakery records needed to answer (for example sales, expenses, stock, orders and production for the dates you ask about) are sent to our AI provider to produce the reply. The chat itself is saved only on your phone. When AboBake AI prepares a sale, expense or order for you, nothing is saved until you tap Confirm.
  • Photos of bills and recipes: if you scan a supplier bill or a recipe, the photo you take or pick is sent to our AI provider to read the lines. We do not keep the photo. The lines it reads are shown to you, and only what you check and confirm is saved.
  • Our AI provider: AboBake AI runs on Google's Gemini service or Anthropic's Claude service. On a paid plan, the provider uses the data only to give the answer. If we ever run it on a provider's free plan, that provider's terms may let it use the data to improve its products; for that reason we don't send your customers' phone numbers to Gemini. We will keep this section up to date if the provider changes.
  • We keep a count of how many AI questions you use each day and month, to apply fair-use limits.

Photos you add to products

If you add a photo to a product, the app asks for permission to use your camera or photo library, and uploads only the photo you pick. Photos are stored at a web address that is hard to guess; anyone who has that exact address could view the photo, so please don't use photos that show private information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we save your phone's push address (a random code made by the notification service) against your account, together with the phone type (Android or iPhone) and your reminder times. We use it only to send the reminders and alerts you switch on, and any announcement we send to app users. Notifications go through Expo's and Google's notification services. You can switch notifications off in the app or in your phone settings.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to AboBake Premium, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives a random account ID and your purchase status so the app knows which features to unlock.

Information that stays on your phone

If you use the app as a guest, the bakery profile you enter is saved only on your phone. The app also stores small settings on your phone, such as your language, currency, units, Quick actions, whether you've finished the welcome screens, and your sign-in session.

We don't collect your location, contacts, SMS, call logs or device advertising ID, and the app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To save your bakery records and show them back to you: costs, plans, stock, orders, expenses, reports and charts.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as password reset codes, and the reminders you switch on.
  • To unlock Premium features when you have an active subscription.
  • To answer your questions in AboBake AI and read the photos you give it, if you use it.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We only share it with the service providers that run parts of the app for us, and only as much as they need:

  • Supabase: stores your account, your bakery records and product photos, and handles sign-in.
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you use it), the Gmail service that sends our account emails, Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications, and Google Play for payments.
  • Expo: delivers push notifications and app updates.
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of your subscription status.
  • Google (Gemini) or Anthropic (Claude): runs AboBake AI and photo reading, only when you use them (see above).

When you use "Share on WhatsApp" or "Share another way", the report is sent only to the app and people you choose. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and bakery records for as long as your account exists, so they're there when you come back. When you delete your account in the app, your account, records and photos are deleted straight away. Requests sent by email or from our delete-account page are handled within 30 days. Copies in our service providers' routine backups are removed as those backups expire.

5. Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login and everything linked to it: products, recipes, product photos, stock, production records, sales, expenses, cash counts, staff records, customer orders and dues, and your notification address. You can also delete individual records in the app at any time.

If you can't use the app, follow the steps on our delete-account page. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

To keep your own copy first, use Backup (Premium) to download your records as Excel files.

6. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and each account can only read its own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

7. Children

AboBake is a business tool meant for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing us. We'll reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
